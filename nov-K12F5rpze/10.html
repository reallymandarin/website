10 - 珍珠的历史

The History of Pearls
465 字HSK 3

珍珠是人类最早从大自然得到的“宝贝”，因为它是圆圆的，很光滑，也有自己的光亮。所以珍珠是人们最喜欢的宝贝。珍珠有很长的历史。在西方故事中，维纳斯在贝壳里出生，当贝壳打开时，她身上落下的水珠变成了珍珠。

其实，真正的故事是这样的：很久以前，人们在海边找贝类是为了吃里面的肉。他们打开贝壳的时候，发现里面有圆圆的、闪亮的东西，在阳光下非常好看。于是，人们认为这是上天给他们的礼物。就小心地把它放在头发里，或者衣服上，并把这些宝贝叫作“珍珠”。

原来，珍珠的形成，是因为砂子进入贝类的身体里，贝类为了保护自己，就不断地用自己身体里的东西把砂子包住，包了一层又一层。经过3到10年，就长成了一颗圆圆的珍珠。在人工养珍珠的技术发明之前，珍珠的形成全部靠运气。

古时候，住在海边的人们拿着篮子和长长的绳子，游到海底找贝壳，幸运的时候可以在贝壳里找到珍珠。但到海底找珍珠非常危险，因此，那时候人们得到的珍珠少极了。现在，人们通过养贝类得到珍珠，这种技术让珍珠变得越来越普遍了。价格也很便宜，普通人很容易就能买到。

英文翻译 · English

Pearls are among the earliest “treasures” that people obtained from nature. They are round and smooth, and they have their own shine. That is why pearls have always been among people’s favorite treasures. They have a long history. In a Western story, Venus was born inside a shell. When the shell opened, the drops of water falling from her became pearls.

Actually, the true story is this: long ago, people searched for shellfish by the seaside to eat the meat inside. When they opened the shells, they found round, shining objects that looked beautiful in the sunlight. People thought they were gifts from heaven, so they carefully placed them in their hair or on their clothes and called them “pearls.”

Pearls form when a grain of sand enters a shellfish’s body. To protect itself, the shellfish continually covers the grain with material from its own body, layer after layer. After three to ten years, a round pearl forms. Before the invention of pearl-farming technology, the formation of pearls depended entirely on luck.

In ancient times, people who lived by the sea took baskets and long ropes and swam to the seabed to look for shells. If they were lucky, they might find a pearl inside one. But searching for pearls underwater was very dangerous, so pearls were extremely rare then. Today, people obtain pearls by farming shellfish. This technology has made pearls increasingly common. They are also inexpensive, so ordinary people can easily buy them.
