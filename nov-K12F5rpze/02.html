02 - 将在250年后打开的时间胶囊

The Time Capsule to Be Opened in 250 Years
421 字HSK 3

2026年7 月 4 日是美国建国 250 周年的日子。这一天，美国人把一个特别的 “时间胶囊”，埋在了费城的一个公园里。 人们把各种各样有意思的东西放进一个非常坚固的盒子里，并把这个盒子叫做“时间胶囊”。这个胶囊要到 2276 年才能打开，那时候，美国就 500 岁了。放在时间胶囊里的东西来自美国不同的州，都是一些很有意思的东西。其中最有意思的东西是一台最新的苹果手机 Apple iPhone 17，手机里还保存了一些关于今天的生活的照片和视频。还有一瓶可口可乐，还有 50 个州的州长亲手写的信。另外，还有 200 多件日常用品、体育用品。这些东西都记录着现在美国人的生活。大家希望250年后的人能看到今天的世界。苹果手机代表我们的数字生活，可口可乐是全世界都知道的美国文化符号。这些普通的小东西，就像一封寄给未来的信。 也许有的人会很好奇：今天我们觉得很平常的东西，几百年后的人会怎么看呢？他们能从这个东西中理解我们今天的生活吗？

英文翻译 · English

July 4, 2026, is the 250th anniversary of the founding of the United States. On that day, Americans buried a special time capsule in a park in Philadelphia.

People put all kinds of interesting things into a very strong box and call it a “time capsule.” It will not be opened until 2276, when the United States will be 500 years old. The items came from different states. One of the most interesting is the latest Apple iPhone 17, which contains photos and videos of life today. There is also a bottle of Coca-Cola, letters handwritten by the governors of all 50 states, and more than 200 everyday and sports items. Together, these objects record how Americans live today. People hope that those living 250 years from now will be able to see our world. The iPhone represents our digital life, while Coca-Cola is a cultural symbol known around the world. These ordinary objects are like a letter sent to the future.

Some people may wonder: How will people hundreds of years from now view the things that seem ordinary to us today? Will they be able to understand our lives from these objects?
