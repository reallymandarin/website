03 - 会说话会跳舞的机器鸭

The Talking, Dancing Robot Duck
470 字HSK 3

最近，一只叫 Microduck 的机器鸭变得特别有名。它大约 25 厘米高，只有 800 克重，但不能帮人干活。你买了这个机器鸭以后，还要花时间去“教”它怎样动起来。这只“没有用的”鸭子，为什么还会这么受欢迎呢？我们一起来了解Microduck吧。 先说它的样子。很多机器人看起来像人一样，或者像狗。Microduck 却是一只圆头、长嘴、走路摇摆的鸭子。它走路慢、动作笨，有时还会摔倒。但是这些缺点放在鸭子身上却显得特别可爱。 机器鸭的头上有摄像头和传感器，能看清周围；有两只脚，能走路、转左转右、坐下，摔倒后还能自己爬起来；它还能用嘴巴把东西捡起来。 最重要的特点是，它把训练的机会交给了你。你可以让机器鸭跟着你学习动作。请放心，你既不需要会编程，也不需要太多的电脑的知识。你可以通过说话让机器鸭学习动作。你还可以把你教会它的动作上传到网上，和大家分享。 最后说说价格。399 美元，比很多机器人便宜多了。Microduck 告诉我们：你不一定要买一个什么都会的机器人，但你可以买一个笨笨的，什么都不会的机器鸭，然后像孩子一样教育它。

英文翻译 · English

Recently, a robot duck called Microduck has become very popular. It is about 25 centimeters tall and weighs only 800 grams, but it cannot do people’s work for them. After you buy one, you still have to spend time teaching it how to move. Why is this “useless” duck so popular? Let’s find out.

First, look at its appearance. Many robots look like people or dogs, but Microduck is a duck with a round head, a long beak, and a wobbly walk. It moves slowly and clumsily, and sometimes it falls over. Yet these shortcomings make it especially cute as a duck.

The robot duck has a camera and sensors on its head, so it can see what is around it. It has two feet and can walk, turn left and right, sit down, and get back up by itself after falling. It can even pick things up with its beak.

Most importantly, it gives you the chance to train it. You can teach the duck to follow your movements. Don’t worry: you do not need to know how to program, or to have much computer knowledge. You can teach it movements by speaking to it. You can also upload the movements you have taught it and share them with everyone.

Finally, the price: 399 dollars, much cheaper than many robots. Microduck reminds us that we do not have to buy a robot that can do everything. We can instead buy a silly little duck that can do nothing, and teach it like a child.
