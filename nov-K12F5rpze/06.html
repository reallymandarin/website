06 - 手机在偷听你吗？

Is Your Phone Listening to You?
474 字HSK 3

也许你也遇到过这样的事情。“刚刚我和我朋友说起哪种跑步的鞋子更好，手机就介绍给我了，我的手机是不是在偷听？”

其实大多数时候，手机并没有真的在听你说话。你和朋友说起跑步的鞋子，那么很有可能你刚用手机看过鞋子。你在看视频、购物、看朋友分享的照片的时候，你的每一次停留、点赞、搜索、分享，都会有可能被记录下来。手机的后面好像有一个看不见的“数字影子”。它一直在“想”你喜欢什么。你对一种东西看得越久，“数字影子”就越觉得你喜欢；相反，如果你很快划走了，它就认为你不感兴趣。慢慢地，这个“数字影子”非常了解你爱看什么、可能会买什么、一天中几点看手机最多……甚至它比你家人更熟悉你。

人们对“数字影子”的看法各不相同，有人认为它可以更快地让我们找到有用的信息。因为它了解你，所以它在工作和生活上给你的建议会很准确。但也有人认为“数字影子”会让我们困在自己的想法里。因为无论你是对的还是错的，只要是你常做的、常买的，它就会给你更多。让你觉得：“我是对的”。这就会让人永远生活在自己的世界里，认为这个世界外面的东西都是不好的，都是错的。

英文翻译 · English

Perhaps you have experienced something like this: “I just talked with a friend about which running shoes are better, and then my phone recommended some to me. Is my phone listening to me?”

Most of the time, your phone is not actually listening to what you say. If you talk with a friend about running shoes, there is a good chance you have recently looked at shoes on your phone. When you watch videos, shop, or look at photos shared by friends, your pauses, likes, searches, and shares may all be recorded. It is as if there were an invisible “digital shadow” behind your phone. It is always “thinking” about what you like. The longer you look at something, the more this digital shadow thinks you like it. On the other hand, if you quickly swipe past it, the shadow assumes you are not interested. Gradually, this digital shadow learns what you like to watch, what you might buy, and what time of day you use your phone most—even becoming more familiar with you than your family is.

People have different opinions about the “digital shadow.” Some believe it helps us find useful information more quickly. Because it knows you, its suggestions for work and daily life can be very accurate. Others think it may trap us inside our own ideas. Whether you are right or wrong, as long as something is part of what you often do or buy, it will show you more of it. This can make you feel, “I am right.” As a result, people may live forever in their own worlds, believing that everything outside them is bad or wrong.
