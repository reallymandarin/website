05 - 夏令时和冬令时的由来

The Origin of Daylight Saving and Standard Time
409 字HSK 3

在欧洲，每年10月底的一天，人们会在半夜快到两点的时候，又把时钟调回到一点钟。正式进入冬令时。人们开玩笑地说：“冬令时让我们多睡了一个小时。”夏令时和冬令时是怎么来的呢？ 1907年，英国人维莱特建议在夏天把钟表调快，人们早就起床，多用白天的阳光。从1908年到1915年，他一共向国家提出过五次，但是很多人都不接受，特别是农民。因为农民的工作时间总是跟着太阳。家里的牛、羊、鸡只看太阳，不看钟表，它们每天的生活只跟着自然变化。如果人们把钟表调快一小时，动物吃东西、休息的时间都会变乱。 1916年，在第一次世界大战的时候，德国为了节省能源，第一次改动钟表时间，使用夏令时。不久之后，英国也跟着这样做。战争结束后，很多国家不再改动时间。后来又经过了几次改动。直到1997年，欧洲各国定下统一规则：每年三月最后一个周日，调快钟表，进入夏令时。每年十月最后一个周日，调回原来时间，进入冬令时。这个规则一直到现在还在使用。

英文翻译 · English

In Europe, on a day near the end of October each year, people turn their clocks back from almost two o’clock to one o’clock in the middle of the night. This marks the start of winter time. People joke that winter time lets us sleep for an extra hour. How did daylight saving time and winter time begin?

In 1907, a British man named William Willett suggested moving clocks forward in summer so people would get up earlier and make better use of daylight. From 1908 to 1915, he proposed the idea to the government five times, but many people, especially farmers, did not accept it. Farmers’ working hours follow the sun. Cows, sheep, and chickens do not look at clocks; their daily lives follow changes in nature. If people moved the clocks forward by an hour, the animals’ feeding and resting times would be disrupted.

In 1916, during the First World War, Germany changed the clocks for the first time to save energy by using daylight saving time. Britain followed soon after. Many countries stopped changing their clocks after the war, and the practice changed several more times. In 1997, European countries agreed on a common rule: on the last Sunday in March, clocks move forward for daylight saving time; on the last Sunday in October, they return to the original time for winter time. This rule is still in use today.
