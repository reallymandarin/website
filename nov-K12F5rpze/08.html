08 - 太空狗莱卡

Laika, the Space Dog
523 字HSK 3

1957年11月3日，一只名叫莱卡的流浪狗被送上太空。它不知道，去太空，从一开始就没有回来的计划。

如果莱卡会说话，它会这样和我们讲故事。我没有名字，我是一只在莫斯科街头的流浪狗，冬天很冷，我总是很饿。有一天，有一个人找到我，他摸了摸我的头，还给我吃东西。他叫我——莱卡。后来，我有了我的名字。他们让我坐在一个很小的箱子里，不要动。一开始，我觉得那是一个游戏。后来，箱子变得越来越小。我在箱子里的时间也越来越久。我不能跑，也不能转身，我不知道为什么，但我每天都努力做一个好的小狗。

有一天，他们让我坐在一个箱子里，就跟平时练习一样。但这一次，他们把好多奇怪的东西贴在我的身上。这是今天的新游戏吗？我不明白。门关上了，我看不见他们了。接着，整个世界开始剧烈地震动。声音很大。后来，周围突然安静了，我的身体变得轻轻的，好奇怪。我不知道发生了什么。我飞起来了？我有点害怕。他们还是没有打开门。慢慢地，我觉得越来越热。我想，如果睡一觉，醒来以后，是不是就能回家了……

那只一直相信人类会回来接它的小狗，永远留在了太空。莱卡再也没有回家。2008年，俄罗斯在莫斯科为莱卡建立了一座纪念碑。让人们记住，莱卡帮助人类进入太空。

英文翻译 · English

On November 3, 1957, a stray dog named Laika was sent into space. She did not know that, from the very beginning, there was no plan for her to return.

If Laika could speak, this is how she might tell us her story: “I had no name. I was a stray dog on the streets of Moscow. Winter was cold, and I was always hungry. One day, someone found me, patted my head, and gave me something to eat. He called me Laika. At last, I had a name. They asked me to sit still in a very small box. At first, I thought it was a game. Later, the box became smaller and smaller, and I had to stay inside for longer and longer. I could not run or turn around. I did not know why, but every day I tried hard to be a good little dog.”

One day, they put me in a box, just like during our usual practice. But this time, they stuck many strange things onto my body. Was this a new game? I did not understand. The door closed, and I could no longer see them. Then the whole world began to shake violently. The noise was very loud. Later, everything around me suddenly became quiet, and my body felt light. How strange! I did not know what was happening. Was I flying? I was a little afraid. They still did not open the door. Slowly, I began to feel hotter and hotter. I thought, if I slept for a while, maybe I could go home when I woke up…

The little dog, who always believed that people would come back for her, remained in space forever. Laika never returned home. In 2008, Russia built a monument to her in Moscow, so that people would remember that Laika helped humanity reach space.
