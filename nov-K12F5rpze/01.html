01 - 再见，香港霓虹灯

Goodbye, Hong Kong’s Neon Signs
412 字HSK 3

五颜六色的霓虹灯是香港的标志，大大小小的招牌从楼房的墙面伸出来，站在街上的人们抬头几乎看不到天空。到了夜晚，霓虹灯全部点亮。你会看到一条多彩的街道。可以说，霓虹灯招牌是香港迷人的文化符号。 霓虹灯招牌在20世纪20年代开始在香港出现，在20世纪50年代到80年代增长得很快。1970年，在弥敦道和湾仔周围，你会看到整座楼房都被霓虹灯盖住了。然而，到了今天，你会发现霓虹灯招牌正在慢慢减少，它们正在被LED灯招牌代替。香港霓虹灯招牌减少的主要原因有三个。首先是LED技术的发展，因为LED灯更省电，招牌制作更简单。也可以做出和霓虹灯一样的颜色。其次，霓虹灯的加工需要人手工制作，制作的过程比较复杂，经验丰富的人才能做得好。 最后是安全原因。霓虹灯招牌又大又重，安装在楼房外有时会有安全问题。2008年之后，城市管理更加严格，拆除了不少霓虹灯招牌。 香港霓虹灯招牌正在减少，但它永远是香港文化的一部分，香港的夜晚仍然会十分精彩。

英文翻译 · English

Hong Kong’s neon lights, in every color, are a symbol of the city. Signs of all sizes project from building walls, and people standing on the street can barely see the sky when they look up. At night, all the neon lights turn on, and you see a colorful street. Neon signs are one of Hong Kong’s most charming cultural symbols.

Neon signs began appearing in Hong Kong in the 1920s and grew rapidly from the 1950s to the 1980s. In 1970, buildings around Nathan Road and Wan Chai were covered in neon. Today, however, neon signs are slowly disappearing and being replaced by LED signs. There are three main reasons. First, LED technology has developed: LED lights use less electricity, signs are simpler to make, and they can display the same colors as neon. Second, neon signs must be made by hand. The process is complicated and requires experienced craftspeople.

The final reason is safety. Neon signs are large and heavy, and installing them outside buildings can sometimes be dangerous. Since 2008, city management has become stricter, and many neon signs have been removed. Hong Kong’s neon signs are disappearing, but they will always be part of its culture, and the city’s nights will remain bright and exciting.
