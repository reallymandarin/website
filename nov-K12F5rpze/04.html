04 - 全都是“坏人”！-香港迪士尼万圣节表演

All the Villains! — Hong Kong Disneyland Halloween Show
392 字HSK 3

在迪士尼故事里，不仅有漂亮的公主和英俊的王子，还有不少“坏人”，他们总是在故事里做很多坏事。想看看“坏人”们的表演吗？欢迎来到香港迪士尼公园。 万圣节快到了，在香港迪士尼公园里表演的全都是“坏人”。你看，这是《公主与青蛙》的法西利尔博士。她穿着紫红色的衣服，戴着高高的帽子，总是说着：“一切都可以交易。”你看，《睡美人》里的玛琳菲森来了，她头上长着一只角，手里拿着长长的法杖，上面还有一只乌鸦。她常笑着说，“不要相信美好的童话，黑暗才是真实。”小时候我觉得她很可怕， 但今天看着她，我却开心地笑了。除此之外，《大力士海格力斯》的冥王、《爱丽丝梦游仙境》的红心皇后，他们的表演、衣服、动作都非常有意思。他们好像在说：“万圣节到了，终于轮到我们表演了！” 在乐园里，你还可以开心地和那些“坏人”拍照。 原来，在迪士尼的童话世界里的“坏人”也可以为我们带来很多欢乐。

英文翻译 · English

In Disney stories, there are not only beautiful princesses and handsome princes, but also many villains who do bad things. Would you like to see the villains perform? Welcome to Hong Kong Disneyland.

Halloween is coming, and the performers at Hong Kong Disneyland are all villains. Look, here is Dr. Facilier from The Princess and the Frog. She wears purple-red clothes and a tall hat, and often says, “Everything can be traded.” Maleficent from Sleeping Beauty has arrived too. She has a horn on her head and holds a long staff with a raven on it. She often says, “Don’t believe in happy fairy tales; darkness is the truth.” When I was little, I thought she was frightening, but today I look at her and smile happily. Besides them, there are Hades from Hercules and the Queen of Hearts from Alice in Wonderland. Their performances, clothes, and movements are all very interesting. It is as if they are saying, “Halloween is here; at last, it’s our turn to perform!”

At the park, you can also happily take photos with the villains. It turns out that the villains in Disney’s fairy-tale world can bring us a lot of joy.
