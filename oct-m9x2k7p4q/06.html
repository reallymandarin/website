06 - 花园城市新加坡

Garden City Singapore
420 字HSK 3

新加坡是一个面积很小的国家，土地非常紧张。城市里的高楼越来越多，人们每天看着冷冰冰的墙面和地板，都觉得少了自然的气息和感觉。但土地实在太少了，根本没有地方用来种树种花。2015年的夏天，建筑师陈伟明站在新加坡的一座高楼前，手里拿着设计图纸。他和他的团队有了一个新的想法：既然地面上没有地方种树，那就在大楼的墙上和房子顶上种！

这并不是一件容易的事。植物需要泥土和水，但泥土太重了，高楼的墙壁根本受不了。而且风很大，植物很容易变干。为了解决这些困难，工程师发明了一种又轻又透气的特殊泥土，还在大楼内部设计了收集雨水的系统。下雨的时候收集雨水，不下雨的时候就让水流到植物上。

几年后，这栋大楼建成了。从远处看，绿色的植物和鲜花在大楼的墙上一直向上生长，像一个向上长的森林。住在里面的人们一打开窗户，就能看到绿叶，见到红花。这座大楼成功地把植物邀请回来和人们住在一起，后来，那里越来越多的楼房用了这种方法，让新加坡变成了一个“花园城市”。

英文翻译 · English

Singapore is a small country with very little land. As more and more high-rise buildings fill the city, people see cold walls and floors every day and feel that nature is missing. But there is simply not enough land to plant trees and flowers. In the summer of 2015, architect Chen Weiming stood in front of a high-rise building in Singapore, holding his blueprints. He and his team had a new idea: since there was no room on the ground for trees, they would plant them on the walls and roofs of buildings!

This was not easy. Plants need soil and water, but soil is too heavy for the walls of a tall building to support. The wind is also strong, so plants can dry out easily. To solve these problems, engineers invented a special soil that is both light and breathable. They also designed a system inside the building to collect rainwater and deliver it to the plants when needed.

A few years later, the building was completed. From a distance, green plants and flowers grew upward along its walls, making it look like a forest reaching for the sky. Residents could see green leaves and red flowers when they opened their windows. The building successfully brought plants back to live alongside people. Later, more buildings adopted this approach, turning Singapore into a “Garden City.”
