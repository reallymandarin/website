02 - 乐事薯片的品牌故事

The Brand Story of Lay's Potato Chips
433 字HSK 3

薯片最早出现在1853年的美国，是一个叫乔治·克拉姆的厨师发明的。他在纽约的一个旅馆里做菜。有位客人总是抱怨他做的薯片太厚，多次把他做的菜退回去。乔治非常生气，故意把土豆切得像纸一样薄，然后做成炸薯片。没想到客人十分喜欢，这一种新菜“像纸一样薄”的炸薯片就这样出现了，而且十分受欢迎。

真正把这个很薄的炸薯片做成有名品牌的，是美国人赫尔曼·W·雷。1932年，美国的经济非常不好，23岁的赫尔曼是一个卖薯片的普通人。他开着旧汽车，把薯片放在车后面的箱子里，把薯片卖给南方的很多小商店。1939年，赫尔曼买下了一家生产薯片的工厂，并开了一个公司，他从卖薯片的人变成了生产薯片的老板。1944年，他正式把薯片的品牌确定为Lay’s（乐事），听说，他还是最先在电视上做薯片广告的人。

今天，你在全世界的商店几乎都可以找到乐事薯片。你可能不相信简单的薯片可以做出上百种不同的口味，其中最受欢迎的是黄瓜味、西红柿味，还有小龙虾味、烧烤味等等，是一种人见人爱的零食。

英文翻译 · English

Potato chips first appeared in the United States in 1853, invented by a chef named George Crum. He was cooking in a hotel in New York. A guest constantly complained that his potato chips were too thick and returned his dishes multiple times. Very angry, George intentionally sliced the potatoes as thin as paper and turned them into fried chips. Unexpectedly, the guest loved them very much. This new dish, fried potato chips "thin as paper," came into being and became extremely popular.

The person who truly turned these thin fried chips into a famous brand was the American Herman W. Lay. In 1932, the U.S. economy was very poor, and 23-year-old Herman was just an ordinary salesman selling potato chips. Driving an old car, he put chips in the trunk behind his car and sold them to many small shops in the South. In 1939, Herman bought a factory that produced potato chips and started a company, transforming from a chip salesman into a factory boss. In 1944, he officially established the brand name as Lay's. It is said that he was also the first person to advertise potato chips on television.

Today, you can find Lay's potato chips in almost any store around the world. You might not believe that simple potato chips can come in over a hundred different flavors, among which the most popular are cucumber, tomato, crayfish, and BBQ flavors. It is a snack loved by everyone.
