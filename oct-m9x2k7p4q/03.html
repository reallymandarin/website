03 - 火星上的无人机

The Drone on Mars
420 字HSK 3

2021年2月，火星车带着一架叫“机智号”的小无人机降落在了火星上。这是第一架在其他星球上飞的无人机。在火星上飞并不容易，因为火星上的空气跟地球的空气很不一样。火星上的空气非常薄。为了能飞起来，“机智号”的身体被设计得很轻，只有1.8公斤。它的叶片转得非常快，每分钟要转2400圈，比地球上的无人机快多了。2021年4月19日，“机智号”第一次成功地飞了起来。因为是第一次飞，所以工程师们非常小心。它飞到了3米高的地方，在空中停了30秒，然后又小心地落下来。这短短的39秒让科学家们非常激动。在接下来的日子里，“机智号”飞得越来越高，越来越远。科学家本来只打算让它飞5次，但实际上远远超过了原来的设计。三年里，它一共飞了72次，距离一共17公里。它帮助火星车看清了周围的环境，帮火星车找到了最好的路。

由于叶片坏了，2024年1月，“机智号”结束了它的任务。这架小小的无人机永远停在了火星上。但它拍的照片会一直鼓励人们对火星的探索。

英文翻译 · English

In February 2021, the Mars rover landed on Mars carrying a small drone named "Ingenuity". This was the first drone to fly on another planet. Flying on Mars is not easy because the air on Mars is very different from that on Earth. The air on Mars is very thin. In order to take off, Ingenuity's body was designed to be very light, weighing only 1.8 kilograms. Its blades spin extremely fast, at 2,400 revolutions per minute, much faster than drones on Earth. On April 19, 2021, Ingenuity successfully flew for the first time. Because it was the first flight, the engineers were very careful. It flew to a height of 3 meters, hovered in the air for 30 seconds, and then carefully landed back down.

These short 39 seconds made scientists extremely excited. In the following days, Ingenuity flew higher and farther. Scientists originally planned to have it fly only 5 times, but in reality, it far exceeded the original design. Over three years, it flew 72 times in total, covering a total distance of 17 kilometers. It helped the Mars rover clearly see the surrounding environment and helped the rover find the best path.

Because its blades were damaged, Ingenuity ended its mission in January 2024. This tiny drone remains on Mars forever, ending its journey, but the photos it took will continue to encourage humanity's exploration of Mars.
