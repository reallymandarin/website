一座为动物建的桥

A Bridge Built for Animals

在广西，平陆运河刚刚建成。运河打通之后，当地的交通变得比以前方便很多，运送货物也更快更多了。人们注意到，在运河上有一座特别的桥，它没有坚硬的路面，也没有路灯，它连接的不是村庄，而是两边的大山。原来，这是一座专门为了动物建的桥。有了这座桥，运河两边的树林和草地不再被分开，动物的活动路线不会被完全切断，它们仍然可以找到食物，繁殖后代。

在运河开始使用的时候，这座动物桥也建好了。桥全长约240米，宽20米，整座桥都按照原来山里的树林的环境来设计，非常适合狐狸、野猫、松鼠等动物的生活。为了让桥看起来和山地的环境一样，设计师们把桥的两边和山连接的地方做得特别宽，上面满是和周围一样的树和草，还有动物们喜欢的各种果树。甚至连泥土都是从同一座山挖去的。几个月过去了，我们看到桥上的树木开始长高，最近，桥上开始出现动物的脚印。看来是“甲方”过来验收了。不久我们将会看到，船在桥下开过，动物在桥上跑动。

In Guangxi, the Pinglu Canal has just been completed. After the canal was opened, local transportation became much more convenient than before, and goods are transported faster and in larger quantities. People noticed that there is a special bridge over the canal. It has no hard pavement, nor streetlights, and it connects not villages, but the mountains on both sides. As it turns out, this is a bridge specially built for animals. With this bridge, the forests and grasslands on both sides of the canal are no longer separated, and animals' activity routes will not be completely cut off, allowing them to still find food and reproduce.

When the canal came into use, this animal bridge was also completed. The bridge is about 240 meters long and 20 meters wide. The entire bridge was designed according to the original forest environment in the mountains, making it very suitable for the life of animals such as foxes, wild cats, and squirrels. In order to make the bridge look the same as the mountain environment, designers made the connection areas on both sides of the bridge particularly wide, planting them full of trees and grass that are the same as those around, as well as various fruit trees that animals like. Even the soil was dug from the same mountain. Months passed, and we saw the trees on the bridge starting to grow taller. Recently, animal footprints have begun to appear on the bridge. It seems the "clients" have come for inspection. Soon we will see ships passing under the bridge and animals running on top of it.

语言点 (Language Points)

刚刚(gāng gāng - just did something -HSK4-01)
副词，表示动作或情况发生不久。 Adverb, indicating that an action or situation happened just a moment ago.
例①：在广西，平陆运河刚刚建成。
In Guangxi, the Pinglu Canal has just been completed.
例②：我们刚刚学完第三课。
We have just finished studying Lesson 3.
和......一样(hé ... yí yàng - same as -HSK3-09)
结构：A + 和 + B + 一样。用来比较两者相同。 Structure: A + 和 + B + 一样. Used to indicate that two things are the same.
例①：为了让桥看起来和山地的环境一样，设计师们把桥的两边做得特别宽。
In order to make the bridge look the same as the mountain environment, designers made both sides of the bridge particularly wide.
例②：我和你们一样，都不会说中文。
I, the same as you, cannot speak Chinese.
